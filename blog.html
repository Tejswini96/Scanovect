services

Sectors we serve

3D Inspection

Our 3D Inspection services provide accurate dimensional analysis and quality verification using advanced laser scanning and metrology technologies. We compare scanned data with CAD models to identify deviations, ensuring products meet exact design specifications and industry standards. This helps manufacturers improve quality, reduce production errors, and enhance overall efficiency.

Applications: First Article Inspection (FAI), Dimensional Inspection, Quality Control, CAD Comparison, Manufacturing Validation

Reverse Engineering

Our Reverse Engineering services convert existing physical components into accurate 3D CAD models, even when original design data is unavailable. This enables product redesign, manufacturing improvements, component replacement, and design optimization while reducing development time and costs.

Applications: CAD Model Creation, Legacy Part Recreation, Product Improvement, Design Modification, Manufacturing Support

Sculpture Scanning

Our Sculpture Scanning services digitally capture sculptures, statues, artworks, and cultural artifacts with exceptional precision. The resulting 3D models are ideal for restoration, preservation, digital archiving, replication, and exhibition purposes while maintaining every intricate detail of the original artwork.

Applications: Heritage Preservation, Museum Digitization, Art Restoration, Replica Manufacturing, Digital Archives
